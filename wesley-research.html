01

Ghost Jobs, AI Screening Failures, and Foreign Intelligence Infiltration via Canadian Government Hiring Platforms

An investigative analysis of how ghost job listings, automated screening bypasses, and adversarial actors exploit weaknesses in Canadian public sector recruitment systems. Maps systemic risks from the intersection of AI hiring tools and national security.

AI Security Canadian Government National Security Ghost Jobs Investigative
zenodo.org/records/15808175
02

AI-Powered Recruitment and the Invisible Attack Surface: Adversarial Exploitation of Automated Hiring in the Public Sector

A structured examination of how adversarial actors, including foreign intelligence services, use AI-assisted recruitment pipelines as an entry vector into government institutions. Evaluates the attack surface created by algorithmic screening tools and proposes detection frameworks.

Adversarial ML Public Sector OSINT Threats Attack Surface Policy
zenodo.org/records/20141041
03

Foreign Intelligence Threats Through Recruitment Platforms: A National Security Perspective on AI Hiring in Canada

A national security-oriented study examining how recruitment platforms become vectors for foreign state actors to identify, target, and potentially infiltrate sensitive government roles. Draws on open-source intelligence methods and case pattern analysis.

Intelligence Threats Counterintelligence Canada Recruitment Security OSINT

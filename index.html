Professional profile

The through-line is propagation.

From data errors in clinical systems to security flaws in dependencies, I look for how a small decision travels through an entire operation.

What differentiates the approach: technical foundations make it possible to understand where data comes from; finance experience makes it possible to test whether an answer is operationally valid.

Key strengths

  • Propagation mindset: sees downstream effects of data, process, and security decisions.
  • Finance-driven automation: connects models and dashboards to reconciliation, controls, and measurable outcomes.
  • AI plus control: validates outputs, monitors drift, and treats guardrails as part of the product.
